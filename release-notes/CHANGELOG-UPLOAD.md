## v2.0.0 - The Vocal Fatigue Update

### ✨ New Features
- **Vocal Fatigue System** - Shouting (CAPS LOCK) can strain your voice!
- **CAPS LOCK Boost** - More uppercase = Greater range + Faster mobs + Longer chase
- **Healing System** - Honey, Milk, Water, and Soups heal your throat
- **Commands:** `/attracthelp`, `/attractstats`, `/attractdebug`
- **Visual particles** when mobs are attracted
- **Action bar messages** (less intrusive)
- **Sound effects** for warnings and healing
- **3 Languages:** English, Portuguese, Spanish

### ✅ Bug Fixes
- `scanCooldownTicks` now works correctly
- `forgetTargetAfterSeconds` now controls mob pursuit time

### ⚙️ Improvements
- Auto cleanup of dead mobs (better performance)
- Thread-safe mob data storage
- More configuration options
